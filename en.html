Handball in Záhorie

Welcome! We are Hádzaná Záhorie – a joint project of three handball clubs from the Záhorie region in western Slovakia, north of Bratislava. Together we run teams from the youngest children all the way up to the men's top Slovak league (Extraliga).

Our clubsTeamsJoin usCome to a matchWhere we playContact

Three clubs, one project

ŠKH Rohožník

Handball club from the village of Rohožník – home of our women's and girls' handball.

HC Záhoráci

Men's handball in Malacky – our first team plays the Slovak Extraliga, together with the second men's team and junior boys.

TJ Strojár Malacky

Handball club in Malacky focused on youth handball – boys, girls and the youngest kids.
